Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Short version: Timezen does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. The app is a client for your own self-hosted SolidTime server — your data stays between your device and the server you choose.

What we collect

Nothing. Timezen has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no backend of our own. We (the developer) never receive your data.

Data you enter

  • Server URL & API token — used only to connect to the SolidTime server you specify. The token is stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and is sent only to that server.
  • Time entries, projects, tags, etc. — read from and written to your SolidTime server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. They are not sent anywhere else.

Where your data goes

All network requests go directly from your device to the SolidTime server URL you enter. Timezen does not route your data through any third-party service, and the developer operates no server that receives it. How your data is handled on the server side is governed by that server's own operator and policies.

Demo mode

If you sign in with the demo/review account, the app loads sample data locally and makes no network requests at all.

Data deletion

Signing out removes the stored API token from your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. To delete time-tracking data itself, use your SolidTime server.

Children

Timezen is a productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
